room's instruments are downloaded from their publishers by
`tools/fetch-samples`, each under its own terms.

## Philharmonia Orchestra sound samples (cello)
https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/
Free to use, including in commercial work; the samples "must not be sold or
made available 'as is' (i.e. as samples or as a sampler instrument)". That is
why this repository never contains them: `tools/fetch-samples` downloads the
archive from the Philharmonia's own bucket and prepares the cello on your
machine. Credit the Philharmonia Orchestra if you publish audio.
